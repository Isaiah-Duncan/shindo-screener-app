This puts a fake earthquake reading on your actual screen. If anyone else can see this display, make sure they know it's a test before you trigger it, a real-looking alert shown by mistake is not a good surprise.
Support
All readings shown here are the Japan Meteorological Agency's own, relayed through third-party services (Wolfx, P2P地震情報). Delivery isn't guaranteed to be instant or complete, a report could stop arriving partway through, including before JMA's final update. For the most current information, also check the JMA website or TV/radio broadcasts.
