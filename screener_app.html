Support

Where the readings come from, and how to tell us when something is wrong.

About the data
Every reading is the Japan Meteorological Agency's own, relayed through third-party services (Wolfx, P2P地震情報).
Map: 「国土数値情報（行政区域データ）」（国土交通省）を加工して作成
On launch the app asks GitHub once whether a newer version exists. No personal data is sent, and nothing is downloaded or installed for you.
Delivery may be delayed or incomplete, and a report can stop arriving partway through. For the most current information, also check the JMA website, TV or radio.
